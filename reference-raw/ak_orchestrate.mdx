## ak orchestrate

Coordinate and inspect agent jobs (process supervision: Darwin)

### Synopsis

Discover runtimes, coordinate artifact-backed jobs, and inspect durable events. External process supervision requires Darwin; historical observation and runtime discovery do not claim process-control support.

### Examples

```
  ak orchestrate start jobs.json
  ak orchestrate status <run-id>
  ak orchestrate resume <run-id> jobs.json
  ak orchestrate stop <run-id>
```
