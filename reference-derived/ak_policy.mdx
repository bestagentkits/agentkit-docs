**Read a project's executable agent policy:** what it forbids, prefers and requires, where each rule came from, and what it answers for one operation. Rules compiled from instruction prose are proposals until adopted, and may only warn or rewrite — never block.

Global flags, output modes, and the base exit codes (`0`–`2`) are shared by every command — see [CLI conventions](../cli-conventions). The sections below list only what is specific to this command.

### Related commands

- [`ak`](./ak) — AgentKit CLI
- [`ak policy adopt`](./ak_policy_adopt) — Promote proposed rules into the active policy
- [`ak policy check`](./ak_policy_check) — Ask the policy what it says about one operation
- [`ak policy compile`](./ak_policy_compile) — Propose policy rules from the project's instruction files
- [`ak policy status`](./ak_policy_status) — Show what the project policy currently enforces
- [`ak policy validate`](./ak_policy_validate) — Check that the policy document is coherent
