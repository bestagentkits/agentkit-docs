Reports the operational store's health, its on-disk schema next to the schema this build reads, and the safe remedy when it is not ready, then lists known derived data classes and their default forever retention posture.

**Effects:** Read-only. Inspects the operational store without creating, migrating, or repairing it, and reads derived data class metadata.
