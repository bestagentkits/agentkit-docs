Shows retention policy resolution, runs one bounded operational ingest sweep, and applies fixed-retention prune for Prunable local tables. Runtime-owned source files are never auto-deleted.

**Who it's for:** Operators managing local derived operational data.

**When to use it:** Before/after enabling fixed retention, or to refresh the operational read model once.

**What changes on disk:** ingest writes operational.db; retention --apply deletes eligible derived rows only.

### Examples

```bash
ak data status --json
ak data retention --class ingestion_runs --days 30 --json
ak data retention --class ingestion_runs --days 30 --apply --json
ak data ingest --json
```
