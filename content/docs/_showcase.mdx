Internal QA page. It renders every component the theme must style so regressions
are visible in one place after a Fumadocs upgrade or token change. Not part of
the published navigation.

## Typography

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Body copy is Geist with an
`inline code` chip, a [brand-accent link](https://agentkit.best), **bold**, and
*italic* emphasis. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> to search.

Vietnamese diacritics smoke test: Cài đặt công cụ dòng lệnh — xác minh binary và
xác thực, khoảng hai phút trên mọi nền tảng.

### Heading level three (Geist semibold)

> Blockquote — headings are Geist in both locales (all-sans); presence comes
> from size and tight tracking rather than a serif.
