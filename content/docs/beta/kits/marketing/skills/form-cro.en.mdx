Use `ak:form-cro` to audit a lead-capture, contact, demo-request,
application, survey, checkout, or quote-request form. It aims for more qualified
completions, not just more submissions, while preserving fields the business,
security, or law needs. Its output includes prioritized findings, a proposed
field design, copy and error guidance, and test hypotheses—not live form changes.

## Choose ak:form-cro for a non-signup form

### Use ak:form-cro when

- You need to understand form starts, completion, field errors, abandonment, or
  mobile differences.
- You want to justify every required and optional field against the follow-up
  process and compliance needs.
- You need recommendations for layout, labels, validation, multi-step flow,
  submit states, or post-submit expectations.

### Choose another workflow when

- The form creates an account. Use the signup-flow CRO workflow.
- The form sits inside a popup and the container behavior is the main issue.
  Use the popup CRO workflow.
- The surrounding page, offer, or traffic quality is the primary problem. Audit
  that broader surface before attributing the result to the form.
- You already selected a testable change. Use `ak:ab-test-setup` to design the
  experiment.

## Prepare the form evidence

Provide the form URL, screenshots, or field list; the form type and audience;
what happens after submission; current starts and completions; field-level
errors and drop-off; device split; and the fields actually used downstream.
Also provide accessibility, consent, privacy, retention, security, and legal
requirements. Remove secrets and unnecessary personal records.

Complete [Onboarding](../../../getting-started/onboarding), and confirm Marketing
Kit is installed for the runtime and scope you are using.

## Understand the review stages

1. **Classify the form.** The Skill identifies the form's purpose, audience,
   qualified completion outcome, follow-up process, and business constraints.
2. **Establish evidence.** It reviews starts, submissions, field drop-off,
   validation errors, completion time, and device differences without inventing
   missing baselines. Missing analytics is a finding: instrument the flow first
   rather than guessing which changes will work.
3. **Audit fields and flow.** It challenges field necessity, required status,
   order, grouping, conditional logic, multi-step behavior, and progress saving.
   Every proposed removal or deferral names the downstream owner and use, or
   marks that information as unknown and requiring confirmation before shipping.
4. **Review interaction quality.** It covers persistent labels, useful help,
   keyboard and autofill behavior, accessible errors, preserved input, loading,
   success, and failure states.
5. **Prioritize recommendations.** Each change cites evidence or is labeled a
   hypothesis, with a priority and a metric that would validate its effect.
   Expected conversion effects are not promises.
6. **Define measurement.** Judge success on qualified completions: valid, usable
   submissions divided by eligible form starts, with qualification and the
   observation window defined. View, start, field, error, submit, and success
   events support diagnosis and bounded experiments.

Field, layout, multi-step, error, button, trust, mobile, and measurement guidance
remains available as needed. The Skill consults at most one relevant reference
per task; it does not require every checklist for every audit.

## Keep consent and implementation separate

<Callout type="warn" title="Less friction must not mean less informed choice">
  Do not remove required disclosures, preselect consent, hide optional status,
  misrepresent urgency, or make refusal harder than submission. Conversion does
  not override privacy, accessibility, security, or legal obligations.
</Callout>

- Ask only for data that has a documented purpose, lawful basis where required,
  retention rule, and owner.
- Do not infer or enrich sensitive attributes without approval and a valid data
  policy. A provider name in a recommendation does not authorize network access
  or data transfer.
- Keep labels visible, errors specific, input preserved, and keyboard-only and
  assistive-technology paths usable.
- Treat phone numbers, free-text messages, payment details, and application data
  according to their sensitivity, not as ordinary conversion fields.
- Do not invent privacy or security assurances, response times, testimonials,
  badges, or other social proof. Confirm claims against the actual policy,
  data flow, and supplied evidence before using them.
- Approve code edits, analytics changes, vendor configuration, publishing, and
  production rollout separately from the audit.

## Review the outputs and evidence

A complete result should include:

- A form audit with evidence or a labeled hypothesis, impact rationale,
  recommendation, priority, and validation metric for every change.
- A justified required/optional field list, order, conditional behavior, labels,
  help text, error copy, submit copy, and post-submit states. Each removal or
  deferral names its downstream owner and use or flags confirmation as pending.
- Mobile and accessibility considerations.
- An event plan that avoids capturing field values unless explicitly required.
- Test hypotheses with qualified completions as the primary outcome, supporting
  metrics, guardrails, and assumptions.
- A clear boundary between recommendations and any separately approved
  implementation work.

## Troubleshoot the workflow

| Symptom | Safe next step |
| --- | --- |
| No field-level evidence exists | Add privacy-reviewed start, error, submit-attempt, and success events before ranking fields by impact. |
| More leads arrive but quality falls | Judge success on qualified completions, not raw submissions, and review whether necessary downstream context was removed. |
| A field is legally required | Keep it, explain why it is needed, and test clarity or timing rather than silently deleting it. |
| Mobile abandonment is high | Verify keyboard type, autofill, tap targets, viewport, validation, and data preservation on real devices. |
| The runtime cannot find the Skill | Confirm target and scope, restart the runtime, then follow [Runtime cannot find a Skill or Agent](../../../troubleshooting/runtime-cannot-find-skill-or-agent). |

## Know the limits

- Field-count and layout rules are hypotheses, not universal effect sizes.
- Completion rate alone does not measure lead quality, user understanding,
  successful payment, or downstream value.
- The Skill cannot verify live accessibility, analytics, compliance, or vendor
  behavior without the corresponding access and evidence.
