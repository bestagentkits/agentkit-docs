<Callout type="info" title="Retired invocation">
  `ak-write` is no longer packaged as a separate Skill. Its work moved to two Skills: [`ak-seo`](./seo) `article` (setup, write, review, visuals, cover, and a WordPress draft) for search-led articles, and [`ak-copywriting`](./copywriting) for headlines, CTAs, and conversion copy checked against its CRO checklist.
</Callout>

Do not invoke `ak-write`. This route preserves existing bookmarks. It does not
describe a packaged identity or a runnable command.

When a Kit refresh reuses a saved Skill selection that names `ak-write`, `ak`
replaces it with `ak-seo` and `ak-copywriting` and prints a notice.
