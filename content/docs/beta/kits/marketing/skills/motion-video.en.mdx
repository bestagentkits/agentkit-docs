Use `ak:motion-video` to make a 1920×1080, 30 fps motion-graphic video where
scene cuts land on the music's beats, reveals land on spoken words, and the
audio mix is measured rather than guessed. The workflow is scripted end to end:
facts, script, generated audio, forced alignment, beat grid, bar-spliced music,
timeline and mix, HTML composition, lint and check, render, remux, and social
encode. It renders through HyperFrames (HTML + GSAP) and does not publish the
result.

## Choose the motion-video workflow

### Use ak:motion-video when

- You need a launch, release, changelog, feature, or explainer video with
  voice-over, captions, SFX, and music synced to the beat.
- You want a restyle of an existing video as a separate project, or a partial
  rework such as new music, re-timed drops, a re-mix, a re-render, or a social
  encode.
- You want one named style profile, or one base style with individual
  dimensions borrowed from other styles for particular scenes.
- Every on-screen fact can be traced to a primary source such as release notes,
  repository docs, or code.

### Choose another workflow when

- You need generative text-to-video footage. Use [`ak:video`](./video).
- The composition is a React or Remotion project. Use
  [`ak:remotion`](./remotion).
- You have a general HyperFrames question or want a plain HTML composition
  without the beat-sync pipeline. Use [`ak:hyperframes`](./hyperframes).
- You need talking-head editing. This Skill does not cover it.
- You want a router across every installed motion or video Skill. Use
  [`ak:motion-graphics`](./motion-graphics).

## Prepare the production route

Pin the outcome before any file is touched: topic and sources, style, duration,
voice language, ending, and signature. English voice-over with Vietnamese
captions is the default. The Skill asks only when the style or the facts source
is unknown.

The full route also needs:

- The `multix` CLI on `PATH`, configured with its own credentials for Gemini
  TTS voice, ElevenLabs SFX, ElevenLabs Music composition plans, and ElevenLabs
  forced alignment. Credentials stay in multix's config; the Skill never prints
  or copies them into project files, plans, or reports.
- Node.js to run the project scripts and the pinned
  `npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.77` commands.
- `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` for the audio mix, remux, loudness and black-frame
  checks, and social encode.
- Python with NumPy and SciPy for the beat-grid and audio checks, and PyYAML
  for `scripts/resolve-style.py`.
- A writable project and enough storage for generated WAV and MP3 audio,
  snapshots, and 1080p MP4 renders.

| Runtime | Invocation | Availability boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claude Code | `/ak:motion-video ...` | Native delivery is the default; explicit plugin delivery is also supported. Full production still depends on multix, provider access, Node, Python, and FFmpeg in the session environment. |
| Cursor | `/ak:motion-video ...` | Slash invocation is user-verified. Browser, preview, and supporting tool behavior can vary by setup. |

### Pick or mix a style

Every look is a style profile: a compact motion-design grammar for palette,
typography, layout, material, texture, camera, transitions, pacing, beat
response, UI choreography, diagrams, character motion, captions, and sound.
`references/styles/index.yaml` lists about 60 of them. Brand names such as
Apple, Nike, Linear, or NVIDIA are aliases that lead to a canonical motion
language (for example, Apple-like leads to `cinematic-product-launch`); the
output never uses a brand's logos, fonts, or assets.

- **One style:** name it, for example "comic style" for `comic-multiverse`.
- **Mixed:** keep one base and borrow individual dimensions for particular
  scenes. `python scripts/resolve-style.py resolve data/style.json --script data/script.json`
  resolves the mix into a per-scene table that goes into the plan.

Three styles also ship a detailed reference: `comic-multiverse`,
`glass-keynote`, and `cinematic-product-launch`. The working projects behind
their demos are not bundled; every new video starts from the Skill's
composition skeleton plus the resolved style.

## Follow the production stages

1. **Pin the outcome.** Topic, sources, base style and any layers, duration,
   voice language, ending, and signature are recorded.
2. **Scaffold the project.** The Skill creates `assets/videos/<slug>/` and a
   plan under `plans/`, then copies the template scripts, example data files,
   `hyperframes.json`, and the composition skeleton. A finished edition is never
   overwritten; a restyle is a new project directory.
3. **Write facts and script.** Facts come only from primary sources, with a short
   evidence report before scripting. `data/script.json` holds the scenes and lines.
4. **Generate and align audio.** multix produces the voice-over, SFX, and music;
   ElevenLabs forced alignment maps spoken words to timing.
5. **Fit the beat and arrange music.** `fit-beat-grid.py` reports BPM and a
   per-bar table; bars are spliced so drops land where the script needs them,
   and `verify-arrangement.py` checks the splice.
6. **Build the timeline and mix.** `build-timeline` renders the ducked,
   loudness-normalized mix; `measure-mix-balance.py` reports music under speech.
7. **Build and validate the composition.** `index.html` follows the composition
   contract and the resolved style, then passes `hyperframes lint`, `check`, and
   scene `snapshot` review.
8. **Render, remux, and encode.** HyperFrames renders the video, the measured mix
   is remuxed back in, and an optional social encode produces a smaller file.

## Keep generation, spend, and publishing gated

<Callout type="warn" title="A video brief does not authorize provider spend">
  Voice, SFX, music, and alignment requests go through multix to Gemini and
  ElevenLabs. Regenerating scenes or music creates more provider requests. Approve
  each generation stage under your provider, quota, and billing policy.
</Callout>

| Work category | Normal boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Local mutations | The workflow creates a project directory, plan, data files, generated audio, snapshots, and renders. It does not overwrite a finished edition. |
| Provider and network effects | multix sends script text and audio to Gemini and ElevenLabs; `npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.77` can fetch the pinned package. |
| Processes | `hyperframes preview` runs as a tracked background process that is reused for this project and stopped when the session ends. |
| Publishing and account effects | The Skill produces the file and leaves posting to you. It does not upload, schedule, or post to social accounts. |

Brand aliases select a motion language only. Confirm rights for fonts, images,
music, voices, and any product visuals, and verify every on-screen claim against
the collected sources.

## Review outputs and evidence

A project typically contains:

```text
assets/videos/<slug>/index.html
assets/videos/<slug>/hyperframes.json
assets/videos/<slug>/data/script.json
assets/videos/<slug>/assets/audio/{vo,sfx,music}/
assets/videos/<slug>/renders/<slug>.mp4
assets/videos/<slug>/renders/<slug>-social.mp4
```

The Skill reports measured numbers, not impressions. It is done only when:

- `hyperframes lint` and `check` pass and snapshots of every scene were reviewed.
- `verify-arrangement.py` reports every segment within ±2 ms, and
  `measure-mix-balance.py` shows the music about 4–6 dB under speech.
- `ffprobe` reports 1920×1080, 30 fps, the expected duration, and AAC 48 kHz
  stereo; loudness is about −14 LUFS with peak at or below −1 dBFS, and
  blackdetect finds nothing outside fades.
- `build-timeline` printed no clip, overlap, or missing-word warnings.
- On-screen facts trace to the sources collected at the start.

## Troubleshoot safely

| Symptom | Safe next step |
| --- | --- |
| Runtime cannot find `ak:motion-video` | Confirm target and scope, start a fresh session, then follow [Runtime cannot find a Skill or Agent](../../../troubleshooting/runtime-cannot-find-skill-or-agent). |
| `multix` is missing or a provider call fails | Stop before generation, keep the script and data files, and fix multix or its own credential config. Do not copy keys into the project. |
| Alignment warns that aligned words and script tokens differ | Fix the scene's `en` or `say` text, or regenerate that scene's voice-over, then realign. |
| `verify-arrangement.py` reports drift | Re-fit the beat grid, adjust the arrangement, and re-run the arrangement and timeline steps. |
| `lint` or `check` fails | Fix the composition against the composition contract before rendering. |
| Only visuals changed | Re-run `lint`, `check`, `render`, and the remux; audio does not need regeneration. |
