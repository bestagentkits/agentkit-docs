Use `ak:pricing-strategy` to structure a pricing decision across packaging,
value metric, and price point. The Skill can outline research, compare tier and
monetization options, examine freemium or trial trade-offs, and propose price
tests. Its output is decision support, not an approved price or account change.

## Choose ak:pricing-strategy for a pricing decision

### Use ak:pricing-strategy when

- You need to review tiers, packaging, limits, value metrics, or monetization.
- You are considering freemium, a trial, a new tier, enterprise packaging, or a
  price change.
- You have customer, competitor, usage, conversion, churn, cost, and sales
  evidence that can constrain options.
- You need a Van Westendorp, Gabor-Granger, MaxDiff, or conjoint research plan
  and understand that each answers a different question.

### Choose another workflow when

- You only need pricing-page copy or page conversion review. Use the relevant
  copy or page CRO workflow after the commercial decision is approved.
- You already selected one measurable change. Use `ak:ab-test-setup`, while
  accounting for pricing-test fairness and legal risk.
- You need to edit billing, migrate subscriptions, notify customers, or publish
  prices. Use a separately approved implementation and release workflow.
- Reliable willingness-to-pay or unit-economic evidence is unavailable. Start
  with research rather than choosing a precise price from examples.

## Prepare commercial and economic evidence

Provide the product, market, target segments, go-to-market motion, current
packaging and prices, alternatives, differentiation, usage patterns, conversion,
ARPU, churn, retention, support burden, variable and fixed costs, payment fees,
tax assumptions, refund behavior, contract constraints, and decision goal.
Label currency, billing period, tax treatment, cohort, geography, and evidence
date. Do not provide credentials or unnecessary customer records.

Complete [Onboarding](../../../getting-started/onboarding), and confirm Marketing
Kit is installed for the runtime and scope you are using.

## Understand the strategy stages

1. **Confirm the decision and goal.** The Skill identifies whether the work is
   about growth, revenue, profitability, market movement, or packaging clarity.
2. **Separate the three axes.** It distinguishes what each tier includes, what
   usage or value metric price scales with, and the actual price point.
3. **Review evidence and economics.** It compares customer value, alternatives,
   usage, retention, willingness to pay, costs, support, margin, and risk by
   segment without fabricating missing data.
4. **Choose research methods.** It can structure Van Westendorp price
   sensitivity, Gabor-Granger demand questions, MaxDiff feature preference, or
   conjoint bundle research and state the method's limitations.
5. **Develop options.** It proposes tier structures, value metrics, free versus
   trial approaches, enterprise boundaries, migration choices, and trade-offs.
6. **Plan validation.** It defines conversion, revenue, margin, churn, refund,
   support, retention, fairness, and trust measures before a test or rollout.

## Keep the commercial decision with people

<Callout type="warn" title="A recommendation is not a price change">
  The Skill does not authorize billing edits, subscription migration, discounts,
  sales quotes, customer communication, geographic pricing, taxes, provider
  access, publication, or rollout. Pricing affects contracts, trust, access, and
  business viability; approve it with the responsible commercial, finance,
  legal, product, and operations owners.
</Callout>

- Treat every example price, tier, percentage, and benchmark in source material
  as illustrative until replaced with current evidence.
- Test whether a value metric tracks customer value and remains understandable,
  predictable, operable, and hard to exploit.
- Model gross margin, payment and support costs, usage volatility, discounts,
  taxes, refunds, churn, expansion, migration, and downside scenarios.
- Do not show materially different prices to comparable customers without a
  reviewed fairness, disclosure, and legal basis.
- Avoid deceptive anchors, fake discounts, hidden recurring terms, obstructed
  cancellation, and urgency that is not real.
- Keep survey answers separate from observed purchases; stated willingness to
  pay does not guarantee demand.

## Review the outputs and evidence

A useful result includes:

- A decision statement, evidence inventory, gaps, and dated assumptions.
- Segment and pricing-persona definitions supported by data rather than labels.
- Multiple packaging, value-metric, and price options with trade-offs.
- Unit-economic scenarios, sensitivities, and explicit currency and billing
  periods; no fabricated revenue or conversion forecast.
- A research and validation plan with sample rationale and method limitations.
- Migration, grandfathering, communication, operational, compliance, and trust
  risks.
- A recommended next decision and owner, clearly separated from implementation.

## Troubleshoot the workflow

| Symptom | Safe next step |
| --- | --- |
| The Skill proposes precise prices without evidence | Ask it to remove false precision, show assumptions, and design research or ranges instead. |
| Survey signals and sales behavior disagree | Segment the evidence, review question bias and selection, and prioritize observed behavior without discarding context. |
| A value metric improves revenue but surprises customers | Add predictability, caps, alerts, examples, and fairness guardrails before rollout. |
| A price test could expose unequal prices | Stop and obtain legal, ethical, disclosure, and implementation review; use another validation method if needed. |
| The runtime cannot find the Skill | Confirm target and scope, restart the runtime, then follow [Runtime cannot find a Skill or Agent](../../../troubleshooting/runtime-cannot-find-skill-or-agent). |

## Know the limits

- Pricing research estimates preferences under its design; it does not guarantee
  purchases, retention, revenue, margin, or competitive response.
- Competitor pages can omit discounts, contracts, taxes, services, or negotiated
  terms and may not be comparable.
- The Skill cannot validate billing implementation, tax treatment, contracts,
  or current market behavior without specialist review and current evidence.
- The documented package releases contain identical
  `ak:pricing-strategy` content and invocations.

Continue with `ak:ab-test-setup` for an approved validation hypothesis, or
review the [Marketing Kit overview](../../marketing).
