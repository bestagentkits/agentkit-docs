Use `ak:onboarding-cro` to examine the experience after signup and help new
users reach a defined first-value outcome. The Skill produces an onboarding
audit, proposed flow, checklist and empty-state guidance, supporting copy,
measurement, and experiments. It does not change the product or contact users
unless you separately approve implementation or outreach.

## Choose ak:onboarding-cro for post-signup activation

### Use ak:onboarding-cro when

- You need to define or validate an activation event from retention evidence.
- You want to reduce time or steps between signup and first value.
- You need to review first-run setup, empty states, checklists, tours,
  milestones, triggered email, or stalled-user recovery.
- You have cohort or funnel evidence that can locate onboarding drop-off.

### Choose another workflow when

- The issue is account creation before first login. Use the signup-flow CRO
  workflow.
- You need an ongoing lifecycle email sequence rather than the product flow.
  Use the email workflow.
- You need only one selected experiment. Use `ak:ab-test-setup` after the audit.
- Retention evidence does not support an activation event yet. Start with
  analytics and cohort research instead of declaring an “aha moment” by opinion.

## Prepare product and cohort evidence

Provide the product type, audience, core value, current post-signup sequence,
candidate activation event, cohort retention evidence, activation and
completion definitions, step-level drop-off, device split, and known support
issues. Include consent, accessibility, notification, data-handling, and brand
requirements.

Complete [Onboarding](../../../getting-started/onboarding), and confirm Marketing
Kit is installed for the runtime and scope you are using.

| Runtime | Invocation | Availability boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claude Code | `/ak:onboarding-cro ...` | Native delivery is the default; explicit plugin delivery is also supported. |
| Cursor | `/ak:onboarding-cro ...` | Slash invocation is user-verified; broader runtime parity is not implied. |
| Codex | `$ak:onboarding-cro ...` | The Skill uses native Codex discovery; Hook projection is partial. |

## Understand the onboarding stages

1. **Define first value.** The Skill looks for the earliest behavior associated
   with retention and states when the evidence is only correlational.
2. **Map the current flow.** It follows signup through setup, first action,
   activation, and later retention, identifying step and cohort differences.
3. **Choose the first-run shape.** It compares product-first, guided-setup, and
   value-first approaches against product complexity and user needs.
4. **Design supporting states.** It proposes concise checklists, useful empty
   states, contextual guidance, real progress, recovery, and milestone copy.
5. **Coordinate channels.** It may outline behavior-triggered email, push, or
   human help, but each contact and permission path remains separately governed.
6. **Plan measurement and tests.** It defines activation rate, time and steps to
   activation, completion, cohort retention, adoption, guardrails, and bounded
   hypotheses.

## Protect autonomy and informed choice

<Callout type="warn" title="Motivation must not become manipulation">
  Do not fake progress, manufacture urgency, punish dismissal, hide skip paths,
  request notification permission without context, or make unrelated product
  access depend on onboarding completion. A higher completion rate does not
  justify a dark pattern.
</Callout>

- Use actual progress and explain what each required step enables.
- Make tours, checklists, and optional personalization dismissible; preserve a
  clear route back when the user wants help later.
- Ask for push, email, contact, import, and integration permissions at a moment
  where the value and data use are understandable.
- Keep example or demo data clearly labeled and separable from user data.
- Do not infer long-term retention from activation alone; monitor downstream
  quality, support burden, complaints, and opt-outs.
- Approve product edits, tracking, account configuration, outreach, and rollout
  separately from recommendations.

## Review the outputs and evidence

A complete result should include:

- An audit with evidence, friction, recommendation, priority, and uncertainty.
- A stated activation goal and the evidence linking it to later value.
- A screen-by-screen or state-by-state flow with optional and required steps.
- Draft checklist, empty-state, tooltip, milestone, and email copy where needed.
- Event definitions and cohort cuts for activation, time-to-value, completion,
  retention, adoption, and harm guardrails.
- Test hypotheses with no claimed lift before measurement.
- A clear line between design guidance and separately approved implementation.

## Troubleshoot the workflow

| Symptom | Safe next step |
| --- | --- |
| No activation event is supported | Compare retained and churned cohorts, then label any interim event as a candidate rather than fact. |
| Completion rises but retention does not | Revisit whether the flow reaches real value or merely completes setup tasks. |
| Users abandon a required setup step | Explain its value, reduce unnecessary work, preserve progress, and test timing without hiding the requirement. |
| Notifications drive complaints or opt-outs | Pause the trigger, review consent and frequency, and use downstream harm metrics. |
| The runtime cannot find the Skill | Confirm target and scope, restart the runtime, then follow [Runtime cannot find a Skill or Agent](../../../troubleshooting/runtime-cannot-find-skill-or-agent). |

## Know the limits

- Correlation with retention does not prove that an action causes retention.
- Day-based retention targets and product-type patterns must come from your own
  business context; the Skill does not guarantee a benchmark.
- The Skill cannot verify a live flow, notification system, analytics pipeline,
  or accessibility behavior without relevant access and evidence.
- The documented package releases contain identical `ak:onboarding-cro`
  content and invocations.

Continue with `ak:ab-test-setup` for a selected hypothesis, or review the
[Marketing Kit overview](../../marketing).
