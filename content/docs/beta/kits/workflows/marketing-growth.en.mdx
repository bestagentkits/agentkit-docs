---
title: Run a marketing growth workflow
description: Chain Marketing Skills from evidence and planning through launch, conversion work, content, and measurement.
---

Use this guide when a marketing result depends on several reviewed artifacts
rather than one isolated deliverable. Keep evidence, strategy, production, and
publication as separate gates.

## Before you start

Define the business outcome, audience, market, time window, approved brand
inputs, available evidence, measurement baseline, budget boundary, and account
access. A plan or draft does not grant permission to publish, spend, contact
people, or change a provider account.

## Plan and measure a launch

1. Use [ak:marketing-research](../marketing/skills/marketing-research) to collect
   dated, sourced evidence about the audience, problem, market, and alternatives.
2. Use [ak:competitor](../marketing/skills/competitor) and
   [ak:persona](../marketing/skills/persona) only where the launch decision needs
   deeper competitive or audience evidence.
3. Use [ak:pricing-strategy](../marketing/skills/pricing-strategy) when packaging
   or price assumptions affect positioning.
4. Use [ak:marketing-planning](../marketing/skills/marketing-planning) to turn
   accepted evidence into objectives, audience, message, channels, owners,
   milestones, metrics, risks, and approval gates.
5. Use [ak:launch-strategy](../marketing/skills/launch-strategy) to define launch
   phases, assets, channel roles, readiness gates, and post-launch monitoring.
6. Use [ak:campaign](../marketing/skills/campaign) for the reviewable campaign
   plan, then [ak:analytics](../marketing/skills/analytics) for measurement.

Start research with a decision, not a broad topic:

> Determine whether the evidence supports launching this offer to the selected
> audience in the stated window. Separate sourced facts, supplied facts,
> inference, and unresolved gaps.

Do not pass unsupported claims into planning. Preserve the evidence date,
source links, rejected assumptions, and unresolved questions.

## Improve a conversion step

1. Use [ak:analytics](../marketing/skills/analytics) to establish the baseline,
   segment, funnel step, and measurement quality.
2. Use [ak:funnel](../marketing/skills/funnel) to map the journey and prioritize
   an observed drop-off or friction point.
3. Use the focused CRO Skill, such as
   [ak:form-cro](../marketing/skills/form-cro) or
   [ak:onboarding-cro](../marketing/skills/onboarding-cro), to develop a
   testable change.
4. Use [ak:ab-test-setup](../marketing/skills/ab-test-setup) to define the
   hypothesis, primary metric, guardrails, sample assumptions, duration, and
   decision rule.
5. Return to [ak:analytics](../marketing/skills/analytics) after the approved
   experiment has run.

Stop when tracking is unreliable, the baseline is missing, the selected segment
changed, or the proposed test cannot isolate the hypothesis. Do not replace
missing data with invented conversion estimates.

## Build a content program

1. Use [ak:brand](../marketing/skills/brand) and
   [ak:persona](../marketing/skills/persona) to establish approved messaging and
   audience inputs.
2. Use [ak:content-marketing](../marketing/skills/content-marketing) to define
   themes, formats, distribution roles, calendar, and success measures.
3. Use [ak:write](../marketing/skills/write) to produce scoped copy.
4. Route the reviewed draft to [ak:seo](../marketing/skills/seo),
   [ak:email](../marketing/skills/email), or
   [ak:social](../marketing/skills/social) for channel-specific adaptation.
5. Use [ak:analytics](../marketing/skills/analytics) to compare results with the
   accepted baseline and decision rule.

Keep draft creation separate from publication. The handoff must identify the
approved copy, asset versions, destination, schedule, consent or compliance
requirements, and the person who can authorize publishing.

## Complete the workflow

Use [ak:handoff](../marketing/skills/handoff) when another session or owner
continues. The workflow is complete when the evidence and decision record are
traceable, required artifacts are approved, measurement is defined, and every
external action has an explicit owner and authority gate.
