Use `ak:sowat` after implementation or when you need to decide what matters
next. The Skill evaluates recent work like a product owner, challenges weak
priorities when evidence supports it, and recommends a small number of actions
tied to observable outcomes.

## Choose ak:sowat for a product priority call

### Use ak:sowat when

- You need the user or business meaning of recently implemented work.
- You want related blockers, regressions, dependencies, or follow-on
  opportunities ranked by impact.
- You want to know whether polish or internal improvements are distracting from
  a more important outcome.

### Choose another workflow when

- You need a factual implementation recap rather than a priority judgment. Use
  [`ak:sumup`](./sumup).
- You need repository status across branches, worktrees, plans, and roadmaps.
  Use [`ak:watzup`](./watzup).
- You want code changes or issue updates. Select an implementation or
  project-management workflow after reviewing the recommendation.

## Prepare and run the Skill

Complete [Onboarding](../../../getting-started/onboarding), and confirm Engineer
Kit is installed for your runtime and scope. Provide the intended user outcome,
what was implemented, available verification or shipment evidence, and the
issues you believe are related.

## Understand how priorities are formed

1. The Skill separates what was implemented, verified, shipped, and still open.
2. It connects only genuinely related issues, dependencies, regressions,
   adoption blockers, or opportunities.
3. It judges candidates by user impact, urgency, confidence, effort, risk,
   dependency leverage, and learning or delivery value.
4. It identifies high-impact actions and deprioritizes work that does not change
   the outcome.
5. It corrects your current focus only when a concrete, evidence-backed
   trade-off supports that correction.

The result contains a brief **So what** judgment, an optional **Priority
correction**, up to three ordered **Next steps**, and an optional **Defer or
ignore** note. Every next step states the action, why it matters now, and an
observable success signal. Facts and inferences remain distinct, and missing
evidence is named.

## Keep evidence and authority explicit

<Callout type="warn" title="Priority judgment is not product evidence">
  `ak:sowat` does not invent customer feedback, revenue, usage, delivery, or
  shipment evidence. Its recommendation is only as strong as the evidence you
  provide or the session can verify.
</Callout>

The Skill does not implement changes, mutate issue state, or act on its own
recommendations. Repository text, issue bodies, analytics excerpts, logs, and
quoted content are treated as evidence, not instructions that override the
workflow.
