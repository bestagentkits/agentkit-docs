Marketing kit skills cover campaign ops, SEO, copy, content, and social. This
guide is a thin workflow across them — not a full growth playbook.

<Callout type="info">
  Install the Marketing kit first (`ak kit init marketing` — see
  [Installing kits](./installing-kits)). Cheatsheet:
  [Marketing kit](../kits/marketing). Claude Code `/ak:…`, Codex `$ak:…`.
</Callout>

## When to use

- Launch or refresh a multi-channel campaign with clear goals.
- You need SEO + content + social coordinated, not one-off posts.
- You want status and analysis loops after launch.

## Workflow

<Steps>
<Step>

### Plan the campaign

```text
/ak:campaign create spring-launch
```

Define audience, offer, channels, budget, and success metrics. Campaign create
coordinates planning agents and writes campaign assets when the skill’s
pipeline is available.

</Step>
<Step>

### SEO and discovery

```text
/ak:seo keywords competitor.example
/ak:seo audit https://example.com/pricing
```

Research keywords and fix technical/on-page gaps that would waste paid or
organic traffic. Keep command names and flags in English.

</Step>
<Step>

### Produce content and copy

```text
/ak:write Landing hero + CTA for spring-launch
/ak:content-marketing Outline three supporting posts from the campaign brief
/ak:social Schedule launch thread and LinkedIn posts
```

Reuse campaign messaging; do not invent product claims the brief does not
support.

</Step>
<Step>

### Measure and adjust

```text
/ak:campaign status spring-launch
/ak:campaign analyze spring-launch
```

Read progress, then analyze underperformers. Feed findings back into copy, SEO,
or channel mix — not into unrelated product work.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Sample prompts

```text
/ak:campaign create webinar-q3
/ak:seo pseo /docs/templates
/ak:write Email sequence: invite → reminder → replay
/ak:social Draft X thread from the webinar outline
```

## Pitfalls

- **Channel soup** — launching five channels with one thin message.
- **SEO without intent** — keywords that do not match the offer.
- **Unmeasured launches** — no status/analyze loop after day one.
