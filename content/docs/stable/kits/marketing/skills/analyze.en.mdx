Use `ak:analyze` as a broad Marketing report router. It accepts one of six
analysis types—`traffic`, `campaigns`, `conversions`, `funnel`, `content`, or
`engagement`—or the `report` subcommand with `daily`, `weekly`, `monthly`, or
`quarterly`. It gathers data that is actually available, applies the Marketing
analytics framework, and reports findings, gaps, and recommendations.

## Choose ak:analyze for routing, not an assumed connector

### Use ak:analyze when

- You know the supported analysis type or periodic-report cadence you need.
- Approved project data or authorized provider access is available.
- You want a cross-channel overview before choosing a deeper investigation.

### Choose another workflow when

- You need a custom KPI, attribution, cohort, or experiment method. Use
  `ak:analytics` directly.
- You need to add tracking, change an account, or operate a campaign. This Skill
  produces analysis; it does not grant mutation, publishing, or spend authority.
- Your request does not map to a listed type. Define the question and measures
  first rather than relying on the generic name.

## Prepare the reporting contract

Provide the exact type or period, date range, timezone, currency, goals,
comparison period, source list, and metric definitions. The workflow can use
project data and may fetch GA4 or Search Console evidence when a compatible MCP,
credentials, permissions, and current provider behavior exist. Provider names
in the Skill are not proof that a connector is configured.

Minimize personal data. Account, property, campaign, page, query, email, CRM,
and conversion identifiers can expose people or business-sensitive activity.
Use aggregate exports, redact secrets and direct identifiers, and record
sampling, consent, retention, and missing-source limits.

## Understand the stages

1. Parse the analysis type or `report` period.
2. Inventory available sources and distinguish fetched, supplied, and missing data.
3. Apply type-specific measures: traffic sources and trends; campaign cost,
   conversion, and ROI; funnel stages and drop-offs; or content and engagement.
4. Compare with approved goals and a genuinely comparable prior period.
5. Separate observations, anomalies, assumptions, and recommendations in the report.

## Keep decisions and mutations with you

<Callout type="warn" title="A report is guidance, not operating authority">
  Do not let a recommendation automatically change tracking, budgets,
  campaigns, content, accounts, or live pages. Each external mutation needs a
  separate scope, data review, provider permission, cost check, and approval.
</Callout>

## Verify output and evidence

Standard analysis writes `reports/analytics/{date}-{type}.md`. The `report`
route writes `reports/periodic/{date}-{period}.md`. Expect an executive summary,
key metrics, relevant channel or funnel detail, goal progress, source and
period comparison, findings, limitations, and recommendations. Verify formulas,
totals, attribution windows, identifiers, and citations against the originals.

## Troubleshoot and know the limits

| Symptom | Safe next step |
| --- | --- |
| Type is ambiguous | Select exactly `traffic`, `campaigns`, `conversions`, `funnel`, `content`, or `engagement`. |
| Periodic report does not route | Use `report daily`, `report weekly`, `report monthly`, or `report quarterly`. |
| A named provider is unavailable | Use an approved export or configure access separately; do not invent data. |
| Metrics conflict | Reconcile definitions, periods, timezone, currency, and deduplication before interpreting. |
| Runtime cannot find the Skill | Follow [Runtime cannot find a Skill or Agent](../../../troubleshooting/runtime-cannot-find-skill-or-agent). |

The Skill does not guarantee source coverage, connector availability,
attribution accuracy, statistical significance, revenue, or performance gains.
See [Marketing Kit](../../marketing) and [Runtime adapters](../../../concepts/runtime-adapters).
