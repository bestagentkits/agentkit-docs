Use `ak:free-tool-strategy` to plan or evaluate engineering-as-marketing ideas
such as calculators, generators, analyzers, validators, resource libraries, and
interactive education. It connects a real audience problem to an MVP,
distribution, lead-capture choice, measurement plan, and business assumptions.

## Choose strategy before building

### Use ak:free-tool-strategy when

- You know the product, audience problem, goal, and available resources.
- You want to compare custom build, no-code, or embedded approaches.
- You need an explicit scorecard, MVP, promotion, and measurement plan.

### Choose another workflow when

- You already have an approved specification and need implementation. Use an
  implementation workflow with file ownership and acceptance criteria.
- You need verified keyword volume, competitor data, prices, or provider terms.
  Research current sources first; the Skill must not invent them.
- You need a landing-page or tracking review only. Use the relevant CRO,
  analytics, or instrumentation workflow.

## Prepare evidence and assumptions

Provide the core product, target audience, painful task, desired business
outcome, existing alternatives, technical capacity, maintenance owner, budget,
timeline, and distribution access. Label search demand, lead value,
lead-to-customer rate, customer value, build cost, maintenance, and conversion
targets as sourced facts or assumptions.

If lead capture is considered, define consent, purpose, minimum fields,
retention, deletion, access, security, age or regional restrictions, and
follow-up rules. Do not assume an email address authorizes nurture messages.

## Understand the stages

1. Confirm the audience problem, product adjacency, goal, and resource ceiling.
2. Generate focused tool types and compare existing alternatives.
3. Validate demand, buyer fit, uniqueness, feasibility, maintenance, link value,
   and share-worthiness. The packaged scorecard rates eight factors from 1–5;
   its `25+`, `15–24`, and `<15` bands are heuristics, not forecasts.
4. Define one-function MVP, UX, gating choice, privacy controls, and handoff path.
5. Model economics as scenarios. The packaged formula uses estimated leads ×
   lead-to-customer rate × customer value, then compares build and maintenance.
6. Produce a promotion and measurement plan with stop criteria.

## Separate the plan from external effects

<Callout type="warn" title="A strategy does not authorize a launch">
  Building changes code and storage; no-code or embedded tools can create cost
  and provider dependency; lead capture processes personal data; promotion can
  publish or contact people. Scope and approve each effect separately.
</Callout>

Use truthful value exchange and optional choices. Avoid deceptive gating,
prechecked consent, hidden follow-up, fabricated scarcity, or sharing results
without permission. Review accessibility, security, legal, tax, financial, or
medical implications when the tool enters those domains.

## Verify outputs and evidence

The Skill defines no fixed output path. A complete strategy document includes
concept, audience problem, product fit, sourced SEO opportunity, alternatives,
MVP, build/no-code/embed rationale, lead-capture policy, success measures,
promotion, owner, timeline, assumptions, risks, and stop criteria. An
implementation spec is appropriate only after a decision to proceed.

## Troubleshoot and know the limits

| Symptom | Safe next step |
| --- | --- |
| Scores look precise | Show evidence and sensitivity; do not treat the heuristic as validation. |
| Search or competitor facts are missing | Mark unknowns and run current research before recommending investment. |
| Lead capture has no policy | Keep the MVP ungated until consent, retention, deletion, and follow-up are approved. |
| Build scope expands | Return to the single core function and maintenance ceiling. |
| Runtime cannot find the Skill | Follow [Runtime cannot find a Skill or Agent](../../../troubleshooting/runtime-cannot-find-skill-or-agent). |

The Skill does not guarantee traffic, backlinks, leads, conversion, rankings,
payback, revenue, or product-market fit. See [Marketing Kit](../../marketing).
